# SEO Article Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing every blog article.

## Keyword fit

- [ ] Primary keyword is present in SEO title.
- [ ] Primary keyword is present in H1 or close variation.
- [ ] Keyword intent matches article content.
- [ ] Article is not targeting too many unrelated keywords.

## On-page SEO

- [ ] SEO title is 50-60 characters.
- [ ] Meta description is 140-160 characters.
- [ ] URL slug is short and readable.
- [ ] Only one H1 is used.
- [ ] H2 sections answer real buyer questions.
- [ ] Article includes internal links to product, solution, brand, or contact pages.
- [ ] Article includes a WhatsApp CTA.
- [ ] Article is listed on `blog/index.html`.
- [ ] Article URL is added to `sitemap.xml`.

## Trust and compliance

- [ ] No guaranteed medical or treatment outcomes.
- [ ] No unsupported regulatory claims.
- [ ] No exaggerated product availability claims.
- [ ] Language is clear for clinics, distributors, and wholesale buyers.

## Schema

- [ ] Article schema is valid JSON-LD.
- [ ] FAQ schema is valid JSON-LD when FAQ exists.
- [ ] `datePublished` and `dateModified` are updated.
- [ ] Canonical URL is correct.

## Final QA

- [ ] Links work.
- [ ] Mobile layout is readable.
- [ ] CTA opens WhatsApp.
- [ ] Article uses Sowena tone: premium, practical, modern.
